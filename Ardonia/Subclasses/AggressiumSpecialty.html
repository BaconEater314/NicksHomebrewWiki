Aggressium Specialty

You’ve practiced with Aggressium Songs the most, specializing in their use.

This subclass is designed for the Ardonia setting.

Aggressium Restoration

Your practice with Aggressium Songs helps you restore the energy in your Aggressium Songs faster. Once per day, whenever you finish a short rest, you regain a number of expended Aggressium Song uses equal to half your Proficiency Bonus.

Aggressive Tactics

Additionally, when you choose this subclass, you gain Proficiency in Martial Weapons.

Aggressive Safety

You’ve learned how to bend Aggressium Songs to prevent them from hurting those you don’t wish them to. Starting at 6th level, all creatures of your choice automatically succeed on all Saving Throws against your Aggressium Songs and take no damage from them.

Aggressive Songweaving

Your mastery over Aggressium Songs helps you channel more power into them. Starting at 11th level, whenever you use an Aggressium Song you can expend an additional use of the Song to gain one of the following benefits:

  • You gain advantage on all attack rolls made with the Song while it is active.
  • All creatures of your choice have disadvantage on Saving Throws against your Song while it is active.

This can be done before or after the use of the Song.

Aggressium Master

You’ve become the ultimate user of Aggressium Songs. Starting at 17th level, whenever you use an Aggressium Song, you deal additional damage equal to your level against single targets, or half your level against multiple targets.
