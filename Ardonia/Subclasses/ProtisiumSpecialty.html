Protisium Specialty

You’ve practiced with Protisium Songs the most, specializing in their use.

This subclass is designed for the Ardonia setting.

Protisium Restoration

Your practice with Protisium Songs helps you restore the energy in your Protisium Songs faster. Once per day, whenever you finish a short rest, you regain a number of expended Protisium Song uses equal to half your Proficiency Bonus.

Protective Measures

Additionally, when you choose this subclass, you gain Proficiency in Heavy Armor.

Protective Songs

Your extensive use of the Protisium Songs causes them to be more effective. Starting at level 6, you gain the following benefits:

  • Protisium Songs that provide AC benefits now provide one more AC.
  • Protisium Songs that create barriers with health or give temporary hit points now provide double the amount of hit points.
  • Protovisibility doesn’t break the first time you do an action that would normally break it.
  • Protoheal has an additional 20hp added to the pool.
  • Protoclone now pushes you up to 15ft away and the dice become d8s.

Aura of Protection

Your use of the protective Songs has left a residual effect on you. Starting at 11th level, all creatures within 10ft of you gain a bonus to all Saving Throws equal to your Ability Modifier you use to calculate your Song power (Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma).

Protisium Master

You’ve reached the ultimate level of Protisum Song use. Starting at 17th level, you gain the following benefits:

  • Protisium Songs that provide AC benefits now provide two more AC.
  • Protisium Songs that create barriers with health now triple their health and double their AC.
  • Protisium Songs that provide Temporary Hitpoints now provide triple the amount.
  • Protovisibility now counts as “Greater Invisibility.”
  • Protoheal has an additional 50hp added to the pool.
  • Protoclone now pushes you up to 25ft away and the dice become d12s.
