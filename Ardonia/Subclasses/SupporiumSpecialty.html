Supporium Specialty

You’ve practiced with Supporium Songs the most, specializing in their use.

This subclass is designed for the Ardonia setting.

Supporium Restoration

Your practice with Supporium Songs helps you restore the energy in your Supporium Songs faster. Once per day, whenever you finish a short rest, you regain a number of expended Supporium Song uses equal to half your Proficiency Bonus.

Supportive Tactics

Additionally, when you choose this subclass, you gain proficiency with Heavy Armor and Martial Weapons.

Multiweave

Your mastery over the Supporium Songs allows you to use your Songs multiple times at once. When you use a Supporium Song, you can use it an additional number of times equal to half your Proficiency Bonus at the same time, targeting the same or different targets.

  • A creature can only be affected by Supporspike once per round.
  • Using with Supporekesis allows you to catch multiple weapons/projectiles equal to the number of castings without expending additional reactions.
