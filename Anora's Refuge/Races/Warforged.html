Warforged

The Warforged, a mechanical race of people made with living metal and magic. Warforged have souls, much like living people and can feel pain and emotion. Built as tools, they must find their own purpose in the world.

Creature Type: You are a Humanoid

Size: You are Medium or Small. You make this choice when you choose this race.

Speed: You have a walking speed of 30 feet.

Constructed Resilience: You were created to have remarkable fortitude, represented by the following benefits:

  • You have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned, and you have resistance to poison damage.
  • You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe.
  • You are immune to disease.
  • You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep.

Sentry's Rest: When you take a long rest, you must spend at least six hours in an inactive, motionless state, rather than sleeping. In this state, you appear inert, but it doesn’t render you unconscious, and you can see and hear as normal.

Integrated Protection: Your body has built-in defensive layers, which can be enhanced with armor.

  • You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.
  • You can don only armor with which you have proficiency. To don armor, you must incorporate it into your body over the course of an hour, during which you must remain in contact with the armor. To doff armor, you must spend an hour removing it, which also requires you to maintain contact with the armor.
  • While you live, your armor can't be removed from your body against your will.

Languages: You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language of your choice.

Specialized Designs

Many Warforged are often built in certain ways or for certain purposes.

Porcelianforged

The Porcelian Forged are warforged built with a porcelain-like exterior, giving them a distinctive appearance and certain unique characteristics. They are built to be beautiful, agile, and nimble. Their limbs are also able to twist to degrees physically impossible for other Warforged and humans.

Nimble: You have proficiency in the Acrobatics skill and +5 to your movement speed.

Contortionist: You have advantage on saving throws to resist and escape being grappled and restrained.

Steelforged

The Steelforged are warforged built with a steel-like exterior, giving them a distinctive appearance and certain unique characteristics. They are built to be strong, durable, and resilient.

Steel Strength: You have proficiency in the Athletics skill. Your Carrying Capacity is doubled, as well as your push/drag/lift.

Heavy Weight: You have advantage on saving throws against being forcibly moved. Even when forcibly moved, you only move half the distance.

Woodforged

Woodforged are warforged built with a wood-like exterior, giving them a distinctive appearance and certain unique characteristics. They are built to be in tune with nature and possess a deep connection to the natural world.

Nature Attunned: You have proficiency in the Nature and Stealth skills.

Natural Blend: You have advantage on Stealth checks made to hide in plant-like areas.
