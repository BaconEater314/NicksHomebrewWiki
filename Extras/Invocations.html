Additional Eldritch Invocations

Breath of Night

You can cast Fog Cloud at will, without expending a spell slot.

Beshadowed Blast

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 11

Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with that cantrip, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On failure, the creature is Blinded until the end of its next turn.

Bewitching Blast

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 17

Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with that cantrip, you can force it to make a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On failure, the creature is Confused until the end of its next turn.

Brimstone Blast

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 5 (incompatible with Hellrime Blast)

Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. This cantrip now deals only Fire damage. When you damage a creature with this cantrip, it is lit on fire. A creature that is on fire takes Fire damage equal to your Charisma modifier at the start of its turn until it or another creature takes an action to put itself out.

This effect lasts for one minute. Each time you hit a creature with this cantrip, it resets the timer.

This invocation is incompatible with all Eldritch invocations that change the damage type.

Close Range Blast

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip.

You’ve learned how to make the best of enemies close to you. Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. You can now cast it as a melee spell against targets within 5 feet.

Eldritch Chain

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 5

You've learned how to bounce your cantrips to nearby enemies. Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. Once per turn, When you roll the maximum damage on one of the dice for that cantrip or score a Critical Hit, it bounces to another creature of your choice within 30ft. Make a ranged spell attack as normal, dealing damage as normal on a hit.

Eldritch Cone

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 11

You've learned how shape your cantrips into short cones to hit multiple creatures. Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. When you cast this spell, you can choose to make it into a 15ft cone. All creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC, taking the damage of the cantrip on a failure or half as much on a success.

When this invocation is used with Eldritch Blast, it creates a number of cones equal to the number of beams.

Eldritch Doom

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 11

You've learned how to create explosive results with your cantrips. Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. When you hit a creature with a ranged attack roll with that cantrip, it and all creatures of your choice within 10 feet of the target take damage equal to your Charisma modifier.

This invocation is incompatible with all Eldritch invocations that change the damage type.

Hellrime Blast

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 5

Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. This cantrip now deals only Cold damage. When you damage a creature with this cantrip its move speed is reduced by 10 until the end of its next turn.

A creature can only be effected by this cantrip once per casting.

This invocation is incompatible with all Eldritch invocations that change the damage type.

Noxious Blast

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 5

Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. This cantrip now deals only Poison damage. When you damage a creature with this cantrip it must make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC or become Poisoned until the end of its next turn.

This invocation is incompatible with all Eldritch invocations that change the damage type.

Pact Weapon Mastery

Prerequisite: Pact of the Blade, Level 5

When you bond a weapon using your Pact of the Blade feature (not conjuring it) you gain Weapon Mastery for that bonded weapon. You retain Weapon Mastery while bound to that weapon until you bind another weapon or unbind it.

Vitriolic Blast

Prerequisite: A ranged damage-dealing cantrip, Level 5

Choose one of your ranged damage dealing cantrips. This cantrip now deals only Acid damage. When you damage a creature with this cantrip it must make a Constitution saving throw against your Spell Save DC or gain the Melted Critical Injury (-2 AC) if it already does not have it.

This invocation is incompatible with all Eldritch invocations that change the damage type.

Voidsense

Prerequisite: Level 11

You gain Blindsight out to a range of 30ft.
