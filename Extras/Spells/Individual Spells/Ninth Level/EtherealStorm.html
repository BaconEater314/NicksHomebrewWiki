Ethereal Storm

9th Level Conjuration Spell

Casting Time: 1 Action

Range: 600ft

Components: V, S, M (A Crystal Sphere and a Carrot)

Duration: 1 Minute (Concentration)

You summon a storm of ethereal energy in a 60ft radius at point you can see within range. As an action on each of your turns and when you cast this spell, you can cause three bolts of ethereal energy to strike a creature of your choice within the radius. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 10d6 damage of a random type on a fail, or half as much on a success.

To determine the damage type, roll a d6 and consult the following:

  • 1: Fire
  • 2: Cold
  • 3: Lightning
  • 4: Thunder
  • 5: Poison
  • 6: Acid

A creature can only be affected by one bolt per turn.

Spell Lists: Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
