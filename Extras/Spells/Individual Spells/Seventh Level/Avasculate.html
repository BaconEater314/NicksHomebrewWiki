Avasculate

7th Level Necromancy

Casting Time: 1 Action

Range: 30ft

Components: V, S

Duration: Instantaneous

You fire a ray of black necrotic energy at a target within range. The target must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 8d6 + 30 Necrotic damage and becoming stunned on a fail as their body purges their blood and vital fluids through their skin. On a success, the target takes half damage and is not stunned.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the damage increases by 4d6 for each slot level above 7th level.

Spell Lists: Sorcerer, Wizard
