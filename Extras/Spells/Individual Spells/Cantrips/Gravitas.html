Gravitas

Gravity Sorcery Cantrip or Dunamancy

Casting Time: Action

Range: Self (10ft Radius)

Components: V, S

Duration: Instantaneous

You slam the ground, creating a burst of gravitational energy that radiates out from you. Each creature in a 10-foot radius must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take 1d10 Force damage and be pulled 5 feet towards you.

At Higher Levels. This spell’s damage increases by 1d10 when you reach 5th level (2d10), 11th level (3d10) and 17th level (4d10).

Spell Lists: Artificer, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
