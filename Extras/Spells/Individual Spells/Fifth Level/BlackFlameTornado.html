Black Flame Tornado

Fifth Level Black Flame Incantation or Evocation

Casting Time: Action

Range: Self (20ft Radius)

Components: V, S, M (a weapon wielded by the caster)

Duration: 1 minute (concentration)

You spin your weapon above your head, creating a swirling tornado of black flames that extends out from you in a 20ft radius. All creatures within the radius must make a Dexterity saving throw or take 6d6 Black Fire damage. While concentrating on this spell, you can use your action to cast this spell again without expending a spell slot. If you use your action to do anything else, this spell ends. When this spell ends, it creates one final tornado as if you had recast it using your action.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the damage increases by 2d6 for each slot level above 5th.

Spell Lists: Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
