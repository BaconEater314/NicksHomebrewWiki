Laser Ray

4th Level Evocation

Casting Time: 1 Action

Range: Self (120ft Line)

Components: V, S

Duration: Instantaneous

A ray of fire ignites from your hands, shooting out in a 120ft long, 15ft wide line. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d8 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

This spell leaves a trail of flaming clouds in its wake for the next minute. A creature that ends its turn in the cloud or enters it for the first time (except on the casting of the spell) takes 2d6 fire damage.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 4th.
