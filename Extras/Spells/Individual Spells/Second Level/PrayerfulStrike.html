Prayerful Strike

2nd Level Golden Order Incantation

Casting Time: Action

Range: Self

Components: V, S, M (A weapon wielded by the caster)

Duration: Instantaneous

Make a melee attack with a weapon you are holding. You can choose to use your spellcasting ability modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls of this attack. On a hit, the target takes an extra 2d8 radiant damage. You and all creatures of your choicewithin 15ft of you regain hit points equal to half the radiant damage dealt (rounded up).

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd level.

Spell Lists: Cleric, Druid, Paladin
