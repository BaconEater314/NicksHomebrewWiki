Waves of Darkness

Second Level Gravity Sorcery or Dunamancy

Casting Time: 1 Action

Range: Self (10ft Radius)

Components: V, S

Duration: 3 Turns

You slam the ground, causing a wave of gravitational energy to emanate from you. Each creature of your choice in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take 2d6 Force damage and is push back 5 feet. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed back. At the start of your next 2 turns, the wave emanates again from where you originally cast the spell, causing the same effect on creatures in the area. The spell ends after the wave emanates for the third time. You are not effected by this spell whenever any waves appear.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2nd.

Spell Lists: Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
