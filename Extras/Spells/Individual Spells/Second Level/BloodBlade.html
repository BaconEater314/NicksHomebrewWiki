Blood Blade

2nd Level Blood Oath Incantation or Transmutation

Casting Time: Action

Range: Self (15ft Line)

Components: V, S, M (a weapon wielded by the caster)

Duration: Instantaneous

You swing your weapon, causing a line of blood to shoot out from the weapon in a 5ft wide, 15ft long line. When you first cast this spell, you take 2d4 necrotic damage. Each creature in the line must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 4d6 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Creatures take Blood Loss equal to half the damage taken. If you benefit from Extra Attack or similar features, you can cast this spell again without expending a spell slot for as many attacks as you can make.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 2d6 for each slot level above 2nd, but you take 1d4 more necrotic damage.
