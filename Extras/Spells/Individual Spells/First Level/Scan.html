Scan

1st Level Divination

Casting Time: Bonus Action

Range: 60ft

Components: V

Duration: 1 minute

You target a creature within range to try to learn about how high their vitality is. The target must make a Charisma Save, on a fail you learn their hit point maximum, how many hit points they have remaining, and if they have any abilities that may give them more hit points (such as Mythic phases), and how much that ability restores. During this, you know precisely how many hit points the creature has left and can freely recieve that information.

This lasts for one minute or until the creature dies.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target an additional creature within range per spell slot above 1st.

Spell Lists: Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Keyblade Master, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
