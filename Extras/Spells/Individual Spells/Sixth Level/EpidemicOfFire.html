Epidemic of Fire

6th Level Evocation Spell

Casting Time: 1 Action

Range: 120ft

Components: V, S

Duration: Instantaneous

You create a stream of fire that erupts from your hands to a target of your choice within range. The stream then leaps out to another target within 30ft of the first, and then to another within 30ft of the second, chaining up to 4 times. A target can be a creature or an object and can be targeted by one one of the streams.

A target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 12d6 fire damage on a fail, or half as much on a success.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, one additional stream jumps from the previous target to another target within 30ft for each slot level above 6th.

Spell Lists: Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
