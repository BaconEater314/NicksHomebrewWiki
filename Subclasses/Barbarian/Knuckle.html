Barbarian: Path of the Knuckle Duster

The Knuckle Duster Barbarian, a form of warrior that emerged from the illegal pit fights, where fighters brawled it out with just their fists or with good ol brass knuckles. These Barbarians excel at using their fists to solve problems, punching their way through anything, as long as it gets the job done. And what’s a couple of bruises along the way, eh? Not like these guys can’t take a punch.

This subclass is designed for the Upside setting. However, it fits in other settings, removing the Bolt Resistance feature for non-lighting-based firearm settings.

Power Fists

Starting at 3rd level, your unarmed strikes deal a d6 Bludgeoning damage on a hit. When using Brass Knuckles, this becomes a d8.

Additionally, you can make an unarmed strike as a bonus action after you take the attack action.

Bolt Resistance

Additionally, when you choose this subclass, you gain resistance to lightning damage when you rage.

All In

Starting at 6th level, you can sacrifice some of your defense to focus on your unarmed strikes. When you enter a rage, you can choose to lose up to your Constitution modifier from your Unarmored Defense, and gain a bonus to your unarmed and brass knuckle attack and damage rolls equal to the AC lost.

You can only do this if you are not wearing armor.

Haymaker

Beginning at 10th level, you excel at taking punishment and dishing it right back out. While raging, as a bonus action you can reduce all damage you take by your proficiency bonus until the start of your next turn (after resistances). If you reduce the damage to 0, you take no damage. The next successfull Unarmed or Brass Knuckle attack you make after the start of your next turn is a Critical Hit and deal additional damage equal to the number of times you were hit since your last turn.

You can do this a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier. You regain all expended uses when you complete a long rest.

Knuckle Down

Starting at 14th level, you have mastered brutal fighting with your fists. Whenever you take the attack action on your turn, you can make three Unarmed Strikes or attacks with Brass Knuckles instead of two.

Showstopper

The Penultimate move of a Knuckler Duster, capable of taking down any sizable opponent. Also starting at 14th level, you can increase your strength to tremendous levels to tackle the largest opponents. Whenever you land an Unarmed Strike or an attack with Brass Knuckles against a creature, you can choose to deal no damage with the hit. Instead, you grapple the target and leap with it to an unoccupied space within 30ft, slamming it to the ground.

The target must make a Strength saving throw against 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength and Constitution modifier, taking 6d6 Bludgeoning damage on a fail, or half as much on a success. For every size larger than Tiny the target is, it takes an additional 2d6 Bludgeoning damage. Creatures 2 sizes larger than you have advantage on this saving throw.

Once you use this ability, you cannot use it again until you complete a short or long rest.
