Custom Wild Magic Barbarian Table

If a result calls for a saving throw, the DC is 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Constitution Modifer.

Proficiency Bonus is shortened to PB.

Roll Effect
01-02 Magic infuses one weapon of your choice that you are holding. Until your rage ends, the weapon's damage type changes to force, and it gains the light and thrown properties, with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. If the weapon leaves your hand, the weapon reappears in your hand at the end of the current turn.
03-04 A bolt of light shoots from your chest. Another creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take PBd6 radiant damage and be blinded until the start of your next turn. Until your rage ends, you can use this effect again on each of your turns as a bonus action.
05-06 Every creature on the map swaps places with another random creature on the map. If a creature is too large to fit into the location it is being swapped too then it does not move, and its partner picks another random creature.
07-08 Your breath is very spicy. All creatures in a 15ft cone directly in front of you must make a Dexterity saving throw or take PBd8 fire damage, or half as much on a success.
09-10 You grow a lashing, spiny tail, which deals 1d8 piercing damage on a hit and has the reach property. If a creature you can see within 10 feet of you hits you with an attack roll, you can use your reaction to swipe your tail and roll a d8, applying a bonus to your AC equal to the number rolled, potentially causing the attack to miss you.
11-12 Your long jump is increased by 10 feet and your high jump by 3 for the duration.
13-14 An intangible spirit, which looks like a flumph or a pixie (your choice), appears within 5 feet of one creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of you. At the end of the current turn, the spirit explodes, and each creature within 5 feet of it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take PBd6 force damage. Until your rage ends, you can use this effect again, summoning another spirit, on each of your turns as a bonus action.
15-16 Your speed increases by 15 feet for the duration (before instinctive pounce) for the duration.
17-18 Your allies have advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature within 5 feet of you that is hostile to you for the duration.
19-20 Until the start of your next turn, every creature’s weapon is turned into a chair which deals 1d4 Bludgeoning damage on a hit.
21-22 A random creature within 60 feet must use its reaction to move its movespeed in a random direction of your choice
23-24 You and 1d4 creatures are transported to a random plane of the DMs choice. A creature returns at the start of their next turn.
25-26 Everyone falls prone.
27-28 Spectral warriors appear when you enter your rage. While you're raging, the first creature you hit with an attack on your turn becomes the target of the warriors, which hinder its attacks. Until the start of your next turn, that target has disadvantage on any attack roll that isn't against you, and when the target hits a creature other than you with an attack, that creature has resistance to the damage dealt by the attack. The effect on the target ends early if your rage ends.
29-30 While you're raging, the first creature you hit on each of your turns with a weapon attack takes extra damage equal to 1d6 + half your Barbarian level. The extra damage is necrotic or radiant.
31-32 You gain resistance to all damage except psychic for the duration of your rage.
33-34 You summon a single cat at the start of each of your turns.
35-36 Each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take PBd12 necrotic damage. You gain temporary hitpoints equal to half of the roll above.
37-38 Each of your hands transforms into a claw, which you can use as a weapon if it’s empty. It deals PBd6 slashing damage on a hit. Once on each of your turns when you attack with a claw using the Attack action, you can make one additional claw attack as part of the same action.
39-40 The Geese
41-42 The nearest enemy drops whatever it is holding (if it is not holding anything, pick the next nearest enemy until one can drop something). That item is now added to your inventory, regardless of the distance.
43-44 Whenever a creature hits you with an attack roll before your rage ends, that creature takes PBd6 force damage, as magic lashes out in retribution.
45-46 At the start of your turn, a random creature within 60 feet can make an extra attack when they take the attack action on a turn.
47-48 Your mouth transforms into a bestial muzzle or great mandibles (your choice). It deals PBd8 piercing damage on a hit. Once on each of your turns when you damage a creature with this bite, you regain a number of hit points equal to your proficiency bonus, provided you have less than half your hit points when you hit.
49-50 You and one random ally swap health. Health that goes over the maximum is converted into temporary hitpoints.
51-52 For the duration of your rage you can make a single melee weapon attack as a bonus action on each of your turns after this one.
53-54 Your skin turns a translucent, shining rainbow colors for the duration of your rage.
55-56 At the start of your turn, a random creature within 60 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you until the end of your next turn.
57-58 You and a random creature in combat swap places on the map.
59-60 You cast Reverse Gravity centered on yourself
61-62 You and a random creature in combat swap places in initiative.
63-64 You target every creature in initiative with the Vicious Mockery cantrip.
65-66 You cast Fireball at 3rd Level centered on yourself.
67-68 Your soul is temporarily cast out of your body, and you see the map, minis, and players with character sheets. You then return, disoriented as if under the effect of the Confusion spell for one turn. (No one will ever believe you)
69-70 You weapon turns into a goose for the duration of the rage. You are considered proficient with the goose which deals PBd6 of bludgeoning damage on a hit.
71-72 Your reach increases by 5 feet, and if you are smaller than Large, you become Large, along with anything you are wearing. If there isn’t enough room for you to increase your size, your size doesn’t change.
73-74 All creatures within 20 feet (including you) must make a Constitution saving throw or take PBd6 poison damage, or half as much on a success.
75-76 At the start of your turn, choose a creature within 10 feet of you. They must make a Dexterity saving throw or take PBd6 Lightning damage.
77-78 Until your rage ends, you are surrounded by multicolored, protective lights; you gain a +1 bonus to AC, and while within 10 feet of you, your allies gain the same bonus.
79-80 You teleport 30 feet above a random creature within 60 feet. You immediately fall if not supported.
81-82 Flowers and vines temporarily grow around you; until your rage ends, the ground within 15 feet of you is difficult terrain for your enemies.
83-84 All creatures have disadvantage on opportunity attacks made against you. You can dash as a bonus action.
85-86 When you start your turn while raging, you deal PB fire damage to all creatures within 10 feet.
87-88 Target a creature within 60 feet. It must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking PBd10 force damage on a fail. If this reduces the target to 0 hp, they become a fine pile of dust.
89-90 When the next round finishes, initiative instead goes in reverse order (until this option is picked again).
91-92 Everyone becomes deaf for the next PB rounds.
93-94 You teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Until your rage ends, you can use this effect again on each of your turns as a bonus action.
95-96 A tree appears in your space, pushing you out of the way in a random direction.
97-98 At the start of your turn, each creature of your choice within 10 feet gains PB temporary hitpoints.
99-00 You transform into a Large sized Goose. You have 15*PB hp while transformed and your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 Bludgeoning damage and are considered magical. When you reach 0 hp as the Goose, you revert to your original form with any damage carrying over and ending your rage.
